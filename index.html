I Am Mindful!

A quick daily diary for how you're feeling — mood, anxiety, sleep, energy, focus. On your phone. Not in a cloud.

Support Privacy GitHub

What it does

📓One entry a dayFive 1–5 ratings plus an optional note. Takes under a minute.
🧭Backfill past daysForgot to log yesterday? Change the date in the editor.
🔒Stays on your phoneSwiftData on-device. No account, no cloud, no servers.
🧠Grounded in PHQ-9 / GAD-7Dimensions modelled on common mental-health self-report items.

The five dimensions

Mood — how positive you felt overall.

Anxiety — how worried or on-edge you were. (Higher = worse on this one.)

Sleep — quality of last night's sleep.

Energy — how much energy you had.

Focus — how well you could concentrate.

Built openly

I Am Mindful! is open source. The full implementation, build instructions, and privacy contract are on GitHub.
